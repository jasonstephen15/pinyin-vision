Getting Started

PinyinVision helps you read Chinese text by showing pinyin and translations instantly. Point your camera at any menu, sign, or subtitle and the app will overlay the pinyin right where you're looking. Save words to build your vocabulary and practice with the AI Mandarin tutor.

Common Questions

How do I scan text? Open the app and point your camera at Chinese characters. The pinyin appears automatically.

Can I save words for later? Yes. Tap any word to save it to your vocabulary deck.

Is my data private? Absolutely. Camera images are processed in real-time and not stored unless you explicitly save them. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
